Jul 1–5

5 days
Drove from Richardson, TX

New Mexico and Route 66

  1. Richardson
  2. Carlsbad
  3. Cloudcroft
  4. White Sands
  5. Socorro
  6. Albuquerque
  7. Santa Fe
  8. Tucumcari
  9. Amarillo
  10. Home

Highlights

  • Carlsbad Caverns: down the Natural Entrance, back up by elevator
  • Mexican Canyon Trestle and the town of Cloudcroft
  • White Sands in the evening light
  • Petroglyph National Monument, the Breaking Bad stops, and Sandia Peak Tramway at sunset
  • Santa Fe Plaza and Loretto Chapel
  • Route 66 murals in Tucumcari, Cadillac Ranch, then Amarillo

Good to know

  • Overnights in Socorro, Santa Fe and Amarillo.
  • Santa Fe to Tucumcari goes direct through Clines Corners, so no backtracking through Albuquerque.

Jul 27–30

4 days, optional 5th
Drove from Richardson, TX

Buffalo River and Branson

  1. Richardson
  2. Ponca / Jasper, AR
  3. Boxley Valley
  4. Branson, MO
  5. Home

Highlights

  • A float on the Buffalo River
  • Hawksbill Crag, the one hike: about 1.5 miles round trip
  • Elk in Boxley Valley at dawn or dusk
  • Birthday dinner in Branson on July 29

Good to know

  • About 955 miles round trip, with a 6 hour drive on day one.
  • The upper Buffalo often runs too low to float by late July. Check with outfitters a few days ahead.
